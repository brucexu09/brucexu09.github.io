Boxun Xu

My research aims to advance efficient and scalable AI through algorithmic innovation in efficient modeling and the design of high-performance machine learning systems. I currently focus on training and inference for multimodal content generation, addressing computational and memory bottlenecks to enable high-quality, long-horizon generation in real time.

I earned my Ph.D. at UC Santa Barbara, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Peng Li (IEEE Fellow). Before UCSB, I completed my M.S. at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, where I had the pleasure of working with Prof. David Blaauw and Prof. Dennis Sylvester. I received my B.S. from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.

My work received consecutive William J. McCalla Best Paper Award nominations at ICCAD 2024 and 2025. I was awarded the Radhakrishnan Nagarajan Family Fellowship at UC Santa Barbara and won the Second Place at the DAC 2026 Ph.D. Forum.

Efficient Generative Modeling

Sparse Forcing: Native Trainable Sparse Attention for Real-time Autoregressive Diffusion Video Generation

Boxun Xu, Yuming Du, Zichang Liu, Siyu Yang, Ziyang Jiang, Siqi Yan, Rajasi Saha, Albert Pumarola, Wenchen Wang, Peng Li

Preprint 2026

Long-horizon video generation needs persistent visual memory without the cost of dense attention. First native trainable sparse attention for autoregressive video diffusion to make video generation both faster and better.

[Paper][Project]

VAR-Q: Tuning-free KV Cache Quantization for Visual Autoregressive Image and Video Generation

Boxun Xu*, Jiaji Lu*, Yuxuan Yin, Zihu Wang, Ziyue Liu, Yu Wang, Zirui Liu, Peng Li

Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026

Large KV caches limit the scalability of autoregressive image and video generation. First tuning-free KV-cache quantization for visual autoregressive generation, evaluated across nine image and video models.

[Paper][Code][Project]

AMS-KV: Adaptive KV Caching in Multi-Scale Visual Autoregressive Transformers

Boxun Xu, Yu Wang, Zihu Wang, Peng Li

AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence (AAAI), 2026

Different layers and image scales need different cache budgets to preserve generation quality. First adaptive KV-caching framework tailored to multi-scale visual autoregressive transformers.

[Paper]

AI and System Co-design

Bishop: Sparsified Bundling Spiking Transformers on Heterogeneous Cores with Error-Constrained Pruning

Boxun Xu, Yuxuan Yin, Vikram Iyer, Peng Li

International Symposium on Computer Architecture (ISCA), 2025

Spiking transformers produce workloads with different activity densities across tokens and time. First heterogeneous HW/SW co-design framework for brain-inspired neuromorphic transformers.

[Paper][Talk]

3D Acceleration for Mixture-of-Experts and Multi-Head Attention Spiking Transformers with Dynamic Head Pruning

Boxun Xu, Junyoung Hwang, Pruek Vanna-iampikul, Yuxuan Yin, Sung Kyu Lim, Peng Li

ACM/IEEE International Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD), 2025

🏆 William J. McCalla Best Paper Award Nomination

Mixture-of-experts expands model capacity through conditional computation, creating a need for parallel, distributed hardware. First 3D distributed and parallel accelerator for spiking MoE and multi-head attention.

[Paper]

Spiking Transformer Hardware Accelerators in 3D Integration

Boxun Xu, Junyoung Hwang, Pruek Vanna-iampikul, Sung-Kyu Lim, Peng Li

ACM/IEEE International Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD), 2024

🏆 William J. McCalla Best Paper Award Nomination

Spiking transformers combine attention with binary spike operations, but require dedicated hardware to realize their efficiency. First dedicated 3D accelerator for brain-inspired neuromorphic transformers.
